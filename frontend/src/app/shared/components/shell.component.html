Unified Dashboard Platform (UDP) Guide

Quick assistance on navigation, data filters, role privileges, and automated report exports.

1. Executive Vertical Switching

Use the top tab navigation to switch between Engineering (TIPS, THMS, Patrol360), Welfare Schemes (TAHDCO Schemes, TELP, TAMS, TOD), and TNCWWB (Workers Welfare Board).

2. Global Filters & Drilldowns

Select Financial Year, Division, and District in the topbar or Geographic Map. Click on any count or status card to drill down into beneficiary and tender name lists with 50-item default pagination.

3. Exporting & Email Reports

Use the Excel, PDF, and Email buttons in the table toolbar to generate and dispatch formatted executive summaries and spreadsheets directly to district managers and engineers.

4. AI Copilot & Voice Support

Click the bottom-right AI Copilot button or Voice Mic to ask questions about civil work status, government orders, scheme eligibility, or trigger MCP data tools in English and Tamil.
